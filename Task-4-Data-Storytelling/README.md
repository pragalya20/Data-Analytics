# Task 4 — Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

## Overview

This task focuses on transforming analytical findings into a business storytelling presentation supported by statistical validation.

The project combines:
- Data storytelling
- Business intelligence insights
- Hypothesis testing
- Statistical interpretation
- Business recommendations

---

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to:
- Understand regional sales performance
- Identify key business trends
- Validate insights statistically
- Support data-driven decision-making

---

## Key Findings

- North America generates the highest revenue.
- Electronics is the strongest product category.
- Credit cards are the most preferred payment method.
- Sales trends fluctuate across months.

---

## Statistical Validation

An ANOVA test was performed to compare revenue across regions.

### Results
- F-Statistic: 4.08
- P-Value: 0.018

### Interpretation
Since the p-value is less than 0.05, the null hypothesis was rejected.

This confirms that revenue differs significantly across regions.
